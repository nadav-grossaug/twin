`input_data/snap_facebook_to_graphml.py` from the published SNAP files;

## Quality notes

- The upstream converter had one real bug (fixed 2026-11-02): alters with no
  friendships among themselves appear only in `.feat`, never in `.edges` —
  they must still be wired to the ego. Kept as a `q_data_integrity` check
  (feature-node referential integrity).
- 8 nodes carry zero features; 1,155 belong to no circle. Flag, don't drop.
- "Degree" means degree within the published 10-ego crawl union, not
  real-world friend count — a censoring statement for the release metadata.

## Sensitivity

Protected entity = the person (node). Sensitive: friendship lists, feature
fingerprints, circle rosters. Degree + a few known edges is a strong
re-identification vector. See [[dp-release]] and `goal.md` for the row-level
DP unit and the declared node-DP gap.
